# Playbook: marketing

Canonical copy lives in the Foundry repo; apps receive it via `install.sh --update`.
Changes come from `radar/reports/` and need owner approval.

## Scope
Positioning, listing and landing copy, the launch plan and the claims list. The agent drafts;
the owner publishes. No agent posts, sends, schedules, buys or contacts.

## Current practices
_Baseline: seeded from general practice on 2026-11-10; not yet verified by a radar run.
Advertising, consumer and direct-marketing rules are read from the source each time._

- Positioning comes before copy: one page naming the buyer, their problem in their own words,
  what they use today and why this instead. If the page cannot be written, that goes back to
  `product-manager`, not into a slogan.
- Every launch has a claims list. Each factual, comparative or superlative statement in any
  copy has a test, a measured number or a screenshot beside it. A claim without evidence is
  removed. `legal-compliance` checks the list before Release.
- Nothing invented: no fabricated reviews, testimonials, user counts, countdowns, scarcity or
  "was" prices. A real testimonial needs the person's permission, recorded.
- Screenshots and videos show the real app at the released version.
- AI-written or AI-generated marketing material is disclosed where the law or the platform
  requires it, and the app's own AI use is described accurately.
- Outreach to people is the owner's act. Electronic direct marketing to an individual needs the
  consent or the exception the ePrivacy rules give; the plan names which, per channel, and
  `legal-compliance` checks it. Business contacts are not automatically exempt.
- Measurement is planned with the launch: what is counted, with which tool, on which lawful
  basis, and whether consent is needed first. No tracker is added by marketing.
- Each channel's own rules are read before its copy is written (store review guidelines,
  platform advertising policies) and the plan links the page read, with its date.
- The repo README and the listing say the same thing about what the app does and does not do.

## Watch list
Advertising Standards Authority for Ireland code; CCPC and EU guidance on unfair commercial
practices, reviews and price reductions; Data Protection Commission guidance on direct
marketing and cookies; EU AI Act transparency guidance; store review and listing guidelines
(Apple, Google, Shopify, GitHub Marketplace) for the channels apps actually use; search-engine
webmaster guidelines.
